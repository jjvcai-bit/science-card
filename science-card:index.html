01 — Mission

Built to remove financial barriers in academia.

The UK is a world leader in research. But its researchers spend too much of their time chasing increasingly hard-won funding instead of doing the work that matters.

Science Card was founded by Daniel Baeriswyl, a former Biomedical Engineering PhD student at UCL, who experienced firsthand the impact of academic funding constraints — and built a way for consumer spending to power scientific research.

Users round up everyday transactions, choose which research projects to support, and follow the work as it progresses. Science Card pledges 10% of its profits directly into university research alongside user contributions.

Science Card was built to remove financial barriers in academia. With Zebec's technology, infrastructure and on-and-off crypto rails, our mission evolves — capable to deliver next-generation stablecoin streaming payment solutions to campuses worldwide.

02 — How it works

A current account that funds breakthroughs.

i.
Open an account
A free Science Card e-account with a Mastercard debit card, an app for financial management, support for domestic and international payments — plus instant transfers between Science Card users.
ii.
Spend as you would
Use the card for everyday purchases. Transactions automatically round up — the difference is funneled into research projects you've chosen to support across UK universities.
iii.
Follow the science
Pick research areas that matter to you — from dementia and cervical cancer to kidney transplants, climate, and aerospace. Receive direct updates from the academics doing the work.

03 — The Zebec Acquisition

Joining Zebec Network. Going global.

Science Card's commitment to academic finance aligns perfectly with our mission to make payments seamless and inclusive. Adding their mission card to our payroll and crypto card offerings creates a trifecta of financial empowerment for diverse users.
Simon Babakhani COO, Zebec Network

London, UK & New York, NY — May 29, 2025. Zebec Network, a Solana-based blockchain payment network, acquired the assets of Science Card, Ltd, the UK university card platform dedicated to simplifying university research finance.

The acquisition adds Science Card's mission-driven card to Zebec's broader card program — which already spans payroll pay cards and non-custodial crypto debit cards. Together they form a trifecta: payroll, crypto, and mission.

For Science Card, joining Zebec means global reach. Zebec's payment infrastructure brings next-generation stablecoin streaming payments to campuses worldwide — closing the loop between consumer spending in the UK and on-chain settlement everywhere else.

For Zebec, the acquisition positions the company across EdTech, DeSci, and FinTech — and gives it a presence in the UK higher-education market, with relationships already established at some of the country's most prominent institutions.
